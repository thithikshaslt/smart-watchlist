# Product

## Vision

Smart Market Watchlist is a market-monitoring application designed to help users build and follow personalized watchlists of financial instruments.

A watchlist can represent different intentions. A user may be actively researching a stock, casually following its movement, learning about markets, or simply tracking companies out of personal interest.

The product therefore should not assume that every user is an investor looking for trading opportunities.

## Core Idea

The application should be **smart through adaptability**.

Different users care about different information. Rather than deciding universally what information is important, the application allows users to define what they want to see and monitor.

Users should be able to shape their watchlist experience through configurable metrics, charts, and other relevant market information.

The same instrument can therefore have different information surfaces for different users.

## User Types

The product should accommodate a broad range of market-monitoring needs, including:

### Beginners

Users who may primarily want:

* Current price
* Price movement
* Basic charts
* Simple market information

### Experienced Users

Users who may want:

* Additional market metrics
* More detailed charts
* Volume and historical information
* A denser information view

### Casual and Hobby Users

Users who may simply want to:

* Follow selected companies
* Observe price movements
* Track a personal collection of instruments
* Explore market information without trading intent

These categories are not rigid user classifications. The application should allow users to determine their own level of detail.

## Product Principles

### User-defined information

The user determines what information is relevant to them.

### Simple by default

The initial experience should be approachable and uncluttered. Advanced customization should be available without making the default experience overwhelming.

### Adaptable rather than prescriptive

The application should adapt to user preferences rather than impose a single way of monitoring markets.

### Reliable information

Market information should clearly communicate its freshness and availability. Stale or delayed information should not appear indistinguishable from current information.

### Explainable functionality

Important product behavior should have clear, understandable reasons. Intelligence should improve usefulness rather than exist as a superficial AI feature.

## Product Evolution

The product will be developed incrementally.

### Foundation

Establish a reliable brokerage-style watchlist experience:

* Discover financial instruments
* Create and manage watchlists
* Organize instruments
* View current market information
* View historical price data
* Persist user preferences and state

### Adaptive Watchlist

Extend the foundation so users can configure the information presented within their watchlists.

This may include selecting and arranging metrics, charts, and other market-data components.

### Meaningful Change

Use the user's configured information as context for determining what has meaningfully changed since their previous visit.

Meaningful change should be grounded in observable market data and user-defined interests rather than assumptions about trading intent.

## Success Criteria

The product should feel like a practical market-monitoring application rather than a collection of experimental features.

A successful experience should allow a user to:

1. Quickly find instruments they care about.
2. Organize them into persistent watchlists.
3. Understand their latest available market information.
4. Return later and identify relevant changes.
5. Configure the information they want to monitor.
6. Use the same application at different levels of market-data complexity.

The product should become more capable as users customize it, while retaining a simple and understandable default experience.
